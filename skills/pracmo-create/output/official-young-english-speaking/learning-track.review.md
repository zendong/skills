# 英语不再先翻译：年轻人的 30 天生活口语

用真实场景短练和开口跟练，从只会看懂走到能回应、能继续、卡住时也能修复对话。

## 核心目标

能够在常见生活、旅行、社交和工作场景中完成简短连续对话，并在听不懂或忘词时主动澄清和换一种说法。

**为什么做：** 解决学过多年英语但真实场景中先逐字翻译、回应过慢、害怕犯错和一听不懂就停止交流的问题。

## 目标路径

### 1. 能看懂但开口迟疑

熟悉常见词句，却经常先逐字翻译或因担心错误而错过回应时机。

- 能理解常见生活场景中的基础问题 (`baseline-1`)
- 面对即时回应时常出现明显停顿或回避 (`baseline-2`)

### 2. 能快速调用常用表达

能够用短句和常用表达块完成请求、确认、感谢、拒绝等核心意图。

- 能在十秒内对五类熟悉场景说出一句清楚回应 (`chunks-1`)
- 能根据关系和场景选择基本合适的语气 (`chunks-2`)

### 3. 能完成三轮对话

不只回答一句，还能用追问、确认或补充信息让对话继续。

- 能完成提问、回应和确认构成的三轮对话 (`dialogue-1`)
- 能围绕同一场景补充至少一条相关信息 (`dialogue-2`)

### 4. 卡住时能修复交流

听不清、没理解或忘词时，能够请求重复、放慢、确认或换词解释。

- 能使用至少三种修复句应对不同卡点 (`repair-1`)
- 小错误不影响意思时能够继续交流 (`repair-2`)

### 5. 能完成两分钟场景交流

能够围绕常见场景清楚表达意图、补充信息、回应追问并自然收束。

- 能围绕一个熟悉场景连续表达约两分钟 (`independent-1`)
- 能根据对方回应调整表达并保持语气合适 (`independent-2`)

## 初始练习

### 第一反应：这句话怎么接

选择简洁、自然并能完成当前意图的英语回应。

1. At a café, the staff asks, ‘For here or to go?’ You want to take the drink away. What is the most natural reply?

   - [ ] A. I go there.
   - [✓] B. To go, please.
   - [ ] C. I am going now.
   - [ ] D. Please go away.

   解析：‘To go, please’直接回答堂食还是外带，简洁且礼貌。

2. A colleague says, ‘Can we move the meeting to three?’ You are available. What is the best reply?

   - [✓] A. Three works for me.
   - [ ] B. I work at three.
   - [ ] C. Three is working.
   - [ ] D. I don't move meetings.

   解析：‘Three works for me’自然地表示三点可以。

3. Someone introduces themselves at an event. Which reply helps the conversation continue?

   - [ ] A. Okay.
   - [✓] B. Nice to meet you. How did you hear about this event?
   - [ ] C. My English is bad.
   - [ ] D. I have no question.

   解析：回应姓名并询问对方如何得知活动，既自然又能继续交流。

4. In most everyday situations, a short clear sentence can be more useful than a long sentence translated word by word from Chinese.

   - [✓] A. True
   - [ ] B. False

   解析：正确。口语首先要让对方理解意图；短句和常用表达块更容易及时调用。

5. You are five minutes late to meet a friend. Write one or two natural English sentences to apologize and give a brief reason.


   解析：评分依据：①明确道歉；②简短说明而非冗长辩解；③语气适合朋友；④小错误不影响理解即可。示例：Sorry I’m a few minutes late. The bus was slower than usual.

### 对话卡住了怎么办

练习请求重复、确认关键信息和忘词时换一种说法。

1. You did not hear the last part of a station announcement. What should you say?

   - [✓] A. Could you say the last part again, please?
   - [ ] B. Your English is wrong.
   - [ ] C. I know everything.
   - [ ] D. Never mind, I will guess.

   解析：该表达礼貌说明没听清，并请求重复，是最直接的修复策略。

2. You know what you mean but forget the word ‘charger’. Which strategy is best?

   - [ ] A. Stop speaking immediately.
   - [ ] B. Say a random Chinese word louder.
   - [✓] C. Describe it as ‘the thing I use to charge my phone’.
   - [ ] D. Pretend you do not need it.

   解析：用‘给手机充电用的东西’描述功能，可以让对方帮助补词并继续交流。

3. Which sentences can help you confirm information you may have misunderstood?

   - [✓] A. So we meet at the east entrance at six, right?
   - [✓] B. If I understood correctly, I should email it today.
   - [ ] C. I’ll just assume it is tomorrow.
   - [ ] D. It doesn’t matter what you said.

   解析：复述时间和询问理解是否正确都能降低误解；直接假定和回避不会完成核实。

4. If you make a small grammar mistake, the safest response is always to stop the conversation and start the whole sentence again.

   - [ ] A. True
   - [✓] B. False

   解析：错误。若意思清楚，可以继续；只有错误影响关键信息时，才需要简短更正。

5. A hotel receptionist speaks too quickly. Write two sentences: first ask them to slow down, then confirm the check-out time is eleven.


   解析：评分依据：①礼貌请求放慢；②明确复述十一点退房；③两句话意思清楚。示例：Could you speak a little more slowly, please? So check-out is at eleven, right?

### 三轮对话与真实场景表达

在餐饮、工作、社交和问路场景中完成连续表达。

1. At a restaurant, your dish contains peanuts, but you told the staff you cannot eat them. Write two or three clear sentences to explain the problem and ask for help.


   解析：评分依据：说明菜里有花生、提到此前的饮食限制、提出更换或确认安全处理的请求；不要求复杂词汇。示例：Excuse me, this dish has peanuts. I mentioned that I can’t eat them. Could you please check and replace it with a safe option?

2. You disagree with a teammate’s plan but want to keep the discussion constructive. Write one or two sentences that show your concern and invite comparison.


   解析：评分依据：①不是直接否定对方；②指出一个具体担忧；③邀请比较或讨论。示例：I see the advantage, but I’m worried about the deadline. Could we compare it with the shorter option?

3. You need to end a friendly conversation because your train is arriving. Which sentence is most natural?

   - [ ] A. Stop talking now.
   - [✓] B. I have to catch my train, but it was great talking with you.
   - [ ] C. The conversation is finished.
   - [ ] D. You talk too much.

   解析：先说明需要离开，再表达交流愉快，语气自然。

4. A useful two-minute self-introduction at a community event may include which elements?

   - [✓] A. Your name and why you came
   - [✓] B. One relevant interest or recent activity
   - [ ] C. Your identification number
   - [ ] D. Your complete home address

   解析：姓名、来意和一个可继续聊的兴趣足够形成自然入口；身份证号和完整住址不应分享。

5. Write a three-turn mini-dialogue in English. Situation: you ask a stranger for directions, they answer, and you confirm whether the place is within walking distance.


   解析：评分依据：①包含礼貌问路；②对方回答与地点相关；③最后确认步行距离；④三轮逻辑连续。示例：A: Excuse me, how can I get to the city library? B: Go straight and turn left at the bank. A: Thanks. Is it close enough to walk?
